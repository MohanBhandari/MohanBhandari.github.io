Mohan Bhandari received his Bachelor's and Master's degrees in Computer Engineering from Pokhara University. He is currently a PhD Scholar specializing in Medical Image Processing at Sirindhorn International Institute of Technology (SIIT), Thammasat University, Thailand.

His research covers explainable AI in medical imaging, neural architecture search, and vision-language models. He engages in international research collaborations and contributes to academic publishing through editorial and peer-review roles.

He serves as Associate Editor for Frontiers in Digital Health and has received multiple recognitions, including an Excellent Foreign Scholarship at SIIT and a Best Research Paper Award.
